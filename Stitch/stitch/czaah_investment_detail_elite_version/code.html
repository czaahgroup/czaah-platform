Investment Thesis

The Indus-K Placer Gold Project represents a rare opportunity to participate in a high-priority natural resource extraction mandate. Unlike traditional hard-rock mining, this placer project focuses on river-deposited gold, significantly reducing CAPEX and environmental impact.

Current geological surveys indicate a sediment density of 2.4g per cubic meter across the primary 12,000-acre lease. Our thesis is built on the convergence of technological efficiency and geostrategic stability, providing a hedge against global currency volatility.

shield_person Strategic Context & CPEC Relevance

Situated at the confluence of the Karakoram Range, the project benefits from direct proximity to the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC). This provides unparalleled logistical advantages, including recently completed heavy-load highway access and dedicated power grid connectivity.
